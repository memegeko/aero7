# Community Code of Conduct

Aero7 welcomes contributors and testers of every background and experience
level. Be respectful, patient, and specific. Critique ideas and code, not
people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and the
publication of another person's private information are not accepted.

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or
contributions that violate these expectations, and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when needed to keep the community safe.

If a concern cannot safely be raised in public, contact the repository owner
privately through their GitHub profile. Do not include credentials or sensitive
security details in a public message; security reports must follow
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

This policy applies in the repository, issue tracker, review discussions, and
other project spaces managed by Aero7 maintainers.
